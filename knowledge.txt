Seven Kids Code Foundation teaches kids coding, robotics, AI, and game development through fun, hands-on classes. We don't just teach tech — we build confidence.

Mission:
Every child deserves the chance to learn real skills. We make it happen through hands-on classes in coding, robotics, and game design.

Who We've Reached:
Over 150+ kids across Ghana are learning to code, build robots, and create games with us.

WHO IT'S FOR:

Age Range:
We train kids between ages 7 and 16. Programs are designed for complete beginners and for children who are already curious about tech.

Experience Needed:
No prior coding or robotics experience required. Most kids start with zero background — just curiosity. We teach from the basics, step by step.

Who Can Register:
Parents or legal guardians register their child. You confirm you are the legal parent or guardian and that the information provided is accurate.

PROGRAMS WE OFFER:

Main Programs (choose 1 or 2 when registering):
- AI, Generative AI & Machine Learning
- Python Programming and App Development
- 3D Printing Model Design
- Web Development and Design
- Game Development
- Robotics

Featured Learning Tracks:
- Python: Master the world's most popular language through fun, real-world coding challenges
- Scratch: Blast off into coding with colorful blocks and create your own animations
- Roblox: Design immersive 3D worlds and learn Lua to bring your games to life
- Arduino: Connect code to the physical world and build your own smart gadgets
- Blender: Sculpt and animate amazing 3D characters and environments from scratch
- Typing Master: Unlock your productivity and master the keyboard with lightning-fast speed

How We Teach:
Hands-on, learning by doing. Kids build real robots, create games, solve problems step-by-step. Creative challenges help kids develop teamwork, creativity, and critical thinking.

Student Project Ownership:
Students retain ownership of the projects they create. We may showcase student work (photos of robots, game screenshots) for educational and promotional purposes unless a parent specifically opts out.

HOW TO REGISTER:

Registration Process:
Parents fill out the online registration form at https://sevenkidscodefoundation.org/register
After submission, the team reviews your registration and contacts you with next steps.

What You'll Need to Provide:
- Student information: full name, date of birth, gender, school name, current class
- Program selection: choose 1 or 2 programs (maximum 2)
- Training type: online or in-person
- Schedule/location preference
- Parent/guardian information: names, phone numbers, emails, residential areas (both father and mother fields)

ATTENDANCE & BEHAVIOR EXPECTATIONS:

Students should:
- Attend classes on time (online or in-person)
- Treat instructors and fellow students with respect
- Use equipment and software responsibly

For online classes, parents are responsible for supervising the child's internet usage.
